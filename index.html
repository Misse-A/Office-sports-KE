How to use this WebApp

After the match Select the drop down as per the instructions

For group events, only fill up to total 5 members and add the rest separately

Upload a picture before submitting. These pictures will be used for internal purpose only

Pickleball Singles is the main even, and NPC Two Rivers venue is available on Tuesday and Friday for free use. Any other location and event will be paid/planned by individuals participarting

To play for the country, remember to record on the United Hero App as well. You can automate by connecting to third party apps like Garmin, google fit etc. However for pickleball can only be recorded as padel

How the points work

Pickleball Singles Winner gets 6 points and participant 3 points.

Any other individual sports gets you 2 points, and group sport activities gets everyone participating 3 points.
